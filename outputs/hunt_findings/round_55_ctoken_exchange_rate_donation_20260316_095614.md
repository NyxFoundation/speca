After thorough analysis of the entire cToken exchange rate mechanism, I've exhausted the attack surface. Here is my conclusion:

NO_NEW_FINDINGS: The classic cToken exchange rate donation attack is structurally impossible in this Sui Move protocol because (1) tokens cannot be sent directly to the reserve — all deposits go through `mint_ctokens` which properly tracks both `cash` and `total_supply`, (2) the exchange rate starts at 1:1 when `total_supply == 0` and only increases monotonically through interest accrual, and (3) `int_div`/`int_mul` truncation in mint/burn always rounds in favor of existing holders/protocol. All potential issues found either overlap with known bugs (repay_fee_rate_misused #057, non_collateral_interest_skip #044, non_collateral_withdraw_oracle #052, emode_stale_borrow #049a) or involve sub-1-unit rounding effects that don't meet Sherlock HIGH criteria (>1% AND >$10 loss).
